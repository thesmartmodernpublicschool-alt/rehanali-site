Admission Process

A simple and transparent process to welcome your child into our school family.

1

Submit Admission Form

Fill out the online form below or collect a form from the school office.

2

Submit required documents for verification by our admission team.

3

Admission Test / Interview

Student may be required to take a short test or attend an interview.

4

Selection

Successful candidates will be informed about selection and next steps.

5

Confirmation

Receive confirmation of admission and join our school family.
