Our Programs

Academic Programs

From early childhood to secondary education, we offer structured programs that build strong foundations and prepare students for future success.

Early Childhood Education

A nurturing start for our youngest learners with play-based learning, basic literacy and social skills development.

  • Playgroup & Nursery
  • Activity-based learning
  • Social & emotional growth

Primary Education

Building strong foundations in reading, writing, mathematics and core subjects with caring teachers.

  • Grades 1 – 5
  • Core subjects mastery
  • Creative activities

Middle School

Transition years focusing on deeper conceptual understanding, critical thinking and independent learning.

  • Grades 6 – 8
  • Subject specialization
  • Project-based learning

Secondary Education

Preparing students for board examinations and higher education with rigorous academics and guidance.

  • Grades 9 – 10
  • Exam preparation
  • Career guidance

Computer Education

Essential digital literacy and computer skills for the modern world through hands-on lab sessions.

  • Computer lab access
  • Basic programming
  • Digital citizenship

Science Education

Practical science learning through experiments, observations and a well-equipped laboratory.

  • Science laboratory
  • Hands-on experiments
  • Inquiry-based learning

English Language

Strong focus on English communication skills – reading, writing, speaking and listening.

  • Reading & writing
  • Speaking practice
  • Vocabulary building

Mathematics

Conceptual understanding of mathematics with problem-solving and logical reasoning skills.

  • Conceptual learning
  • Problem solving
  • Regular assessments

Social Studies

Understanding society, history, geography and civic responsibility through engaging lessons.

  • History & Geography
  • Civic awareness
  • Cultural understanding
